I'm **Cazzy**—a data scientist, hands-on systems builder and CEO of [<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Cazzy-Aporbo/Cazzy-Aporbo/main/docs/assets/wordmark.svg" alt="LOOPCHii" width="86" height="23">](https://github.com/loopchii). My work follows a practical question: how do we keep intelligence useful, accountable and under meaningful human control as it moves through the world?

My starting point was laboratory quality control. It left me with a useful habit: ask what a result can actually support before deciding what to do with it. That question still follows me into data science, AI governance and the things I build.

**Consequential AI** keeps the human stakes in view. My interest in **sovereign systems** is concrete: who controls a deployment, what it depends on, and what remains possible when a connection or an assumption fails.
